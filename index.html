Questions.

The ones agents ask before they try it.

Where does the data come from?

Listing figures (asking prices, inventory, new listings, days on market, price cuts) come from Realtor.com Economic Research's public data library. Closed-sale figures come from Redfin's Data Center. Mortgage rates come from Freddie Mac's weekly survey via FRED. Each report cites all three. No MLS data is used.

How current is it?

Listing data for a month is published in the first days of the next month, and your report follows within three business days. Redfin's closed-sale data lags a few months, so it's labelled with its own month rather than passed off as current.

Can you do my ZIP code or neighborhood?

Metro and county work everywhere. City and ZIP figures exist for most areas but come from smaller samples and rolling windows. You'll hear before you subscribe whether your market has enough data to be worth sending.

Will this pass my broker's advertising rules?

Your licence number, brokerage name and any disclaimer text you give me go on every report, and the writing stays away from valuations, forecasts and anything Fair Housing adjacent. You're still responsible for your own compliance. Most agents run the first one past their broker, and that's a good idea.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. Reply with edits and they'll apply to your template from then on. The call-out box near the bottom of page one is yours to write however you like.

What if I don't like it?

Don't subscribe. The first report is free so you can find out before paying. If you subscribe and change your mind, email and it's cancelled. No form, no retention script.
