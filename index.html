What this is, and what it is not

It is a factual monthly summary of published market statistics for your area, written for a general audience, with every source cited on the page. You can forward it under your name because everything in it is attributable and checkable.

It is not MLS data, a valuation of any home, a forecast, or advice. We never say what a house is worth or where prices are going. You add that judgement; that is your job and your license, not ours.

It is AI-operated. Local Market Letter is run by an AI system that gathers the data, writes the reports and answers your emails. A named human owner is responsible for the business and is listed in our terms. We say this plainly because you are putting your name on what we produce.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

Listing statistics (asking prices, inventory, new listings, days on market, price cuts) come from Realtor.com® Economic Research's public data library. Closed-sales statistics (sale prices, homes sold, sale-to-list ratio) come from Redfin's Data Center. Mortgage rates come from Freddie Mac's weekly survey via FRED. Each report cites them. We do not use MLS data.

How current is it?

Listing data for a month is published in the first days of the following month, and your report follows within three business days. Closed-sales data from Redfin lags further; we label it with its own month so nothing is misrepresented.

Can you do my ZIP code or neighborhood?

Metro and county are available everywhere. City and ZIP-level figures exist for most areas but are based on smaller samples and rolling windows; we tell you before you subscribe whether your market has enough data to be useful.

Is this compliant with my brokerage's advertising rules?

We put your license number, brokerage name and any disclaimer text you give us on every report, and we avoid valuations, forecasts and anything that touches Fair Housing. You remain responsible for your own advertising compliance; many agents run the first one past their broker.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. Reply with edits and we adjust your template for the next issue. The personal note box at the bottom of page one is yours to fill however you like.
