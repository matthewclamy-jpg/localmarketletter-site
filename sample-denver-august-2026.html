What happened in Denver in August 2026

In August 2026, the median asking price for homes listed in Denver was $574,900, down 4.2% from a year earlier.

Buyers had 12,998 homes to choose from during the month (+2.1% year over year), with 4,444 newly listed (+6.4%). The typical listing spent 57 days on the market, and 31.4% of active listings had a price reduction.

On the sales side, the most recent closed-sales data (May 2026) shows a median sale price of $610,000 (+1.7% year over year), 4,110 homes sold, and sellers receiving an average of 99.3% of their asking price, with 28% of sales closing above list.

Nationally, the average 30-year fixed mortgage rate was 6.66% in the latest weekly survey, compared with 6.56% a year earlier.
